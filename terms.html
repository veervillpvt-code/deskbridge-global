Terms & conditions

The simple rules for working together – free trial, payments, cancellation, refunds and confidentiality.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

These terms apply to all services provided under the name DeskBridge Global by Veervill Private Limited, a company registered in India with its office in Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu ("we", "us"). By requesting a trial, accepting a quote or paying an invoice, you ("the client") agree to these terms.

1. Our services

We provide remote back-office services such as data entry, bookkeeping support, invoice and payroll preparation, customer support, e-commerce operations, virtual assistance, social media, lead generation and website updates. The exact tasks, hours and tools are agreed with you in writing (email is enough) before work starts.

We are not a licensed accounting, tax, legal or financial advisory firm. Bookkeeping work is prepared for review by your own accountant, who remains responsible for filings and advice.

2. Free trial

New clients may get up to 5 hours of work free of charge on a real task. There is no obligation to continue. We may decline or limit a trial at our discretion.

3. Fees and payment

  • Prices are shown on our pricing page in US dollars, unless a different currency is agreed in writing.
  • Pay-as-you-go hours are bought in blocks of 10 hours in advance. Monthly plans are invoiced at the start of each month.
  • Invoices are payable by the due date shown on the invoice. Work may be paused if an invoice is overdue.
  • Bank charges, card fees and any taxes in your country are your responsibility, unless agreed otherwise.
  • We may change our prices with at least 30 days' written notice. Changes never apply to a month you have already paid for.

4. Cancellation and refunds

  • Monthly plans run month to month. You can cancel any time by email; the plan ends at the end of the month already paid for.
  • Unused pay-as-you-go hours stay valid for 6 months from purchase.
  • If you are not satisfied with a delivery, tell us within 7 days and we will redo the work at no extra cost. If we still cannot meet the agreed standard, we will refund the fee for that specific work.
  • Fees for work already delivered and accepted are not refundable.

5. Confidentiality

We sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before you share any business data. Everything you share with us is treated as confidential, used only to do your work, and not shared with anyone else except where required by law. Our team members are bound by confidentiality obligations.

6. Access to your systems

You decide what access we get. We recommend giving access through user roles or a password manager, so you can remove it at any time. We will not change account ownership, passwords or payment settings unless you ask us to in writing.

7. Your responsibilities

  • Give clear instructions and the information and access we need.
  • Make sure you have the right to share the data you send us, including your customers' personal data.
  • Review important work (for example accounts, payments or public posts) before relying on it.

8. Ownership of work

Once paid for, all work we create for you – files, spreadsheets, content and reports – belongs to you. We keep no rights to it, other than keeping records needed for our own accounts and legal obligations.

9. Liability

We take care with every task and check work before delivery. To the extent allowed by law, our total liability for any claim relating to our services is limited to the fees you paid us for the month in which the issue occurred. We are not liable for indirect losses such as lost profits or lost business.

10. Non-solicitation

During our engagement and for 12 months after it ends, you agree not to directly hire a DeskBridge team member who worked on your account without our written agreement.

11. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any dispute will first be discussed in good faith; if it cannot be settled, the courts at Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu, India will have jurisdiction.
