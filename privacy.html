Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and how we keep your data and your customers’ data safe.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains how Veervill Private Limited, operating DeskBridge Global ("we", "us"), collects and uses personal information through this website and while providing our services.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, business name, country, the service you choose and the message you write.
  • Emails and calls: what you share with us when you contact us.
  • Client work data: business data you give us to do your work (for example invoices, customer lists or orders). We process this only on your instructions.
  • Billing: the details needed to invoice you. Card payments are handled by the payment provider; we do not see or store full card numbers.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and set up your free trial.
  • To provide the services you ask for, and to send invoices and service messages.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations in India.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your business data for any purpose other than your work.

3. Services we use

Our website is hosted on Vercel. Contact form messages are delivered to our email through FormSubmit, and we use Google (Gmail and Google Workspace tools) for email and documents. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

4. Your clients' personal data

When your work involves personal data of your customers or staff, you remain responsible for it ("controller") and we act on your instructions ("processor"). We use it only for your work, keep it confidential under our NDA, limit access to the team members on your account, and delete or return it when the work ends or when you ask. We can sign a data processing agreement on request.

5. Security

  • NDA and confidentiality obligations for every team member.
  • Access only to the systems and files needed for your work, preferably through your own user roles.
  • Protected devices and accounts, with two-step verification where available.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to 2 years. Client work data is kept only as long as needed for the work, then deleted or returned. Invoices and accounting records are kept as long as Indian law requires.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you. If you are in the European Union, the United Kingdom or another country with data protection laws, you have the rights those laws give you. In India, we follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: deskbridgeglobal@gmail.com · Veervill Private Limited, Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu, India.
