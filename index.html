FAQ

Our office is in Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu, India. The team works in English and Tamil.

Can you work during my business hours?

Yes. Tell us your time zone and hours, and we schedule your assistant to match. For the USA this means our evening and night shift.

How does the free trial work?

Send us a real task. We spend up to 5 hours on it at no cost. You review the result and decide whether to continue.

How do I pay?

We invoice monthly in USD, or in your currency on request. You can pay by international bank transfer or card.

Is there a minimum contract?

Plans are month to month. Pay-as-you-go hours are bought in blocks of 10.

What tools do I need?

None new. We work inside the tools you already use, such as Google Workspace, QuickBooks, Shopify or your helpdesk.
